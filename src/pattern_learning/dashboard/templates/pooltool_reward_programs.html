PoolTool reward programs

Use a learned PoolTool library as the symbolic vocabulary, then either synthesize a DSL reward from a natural-language goal or provide the DSL directly. The page supports the two paper-style experiments: simulated-annealing optimization with dense reward vs binary reward, and value-model training from reward-program labels.
